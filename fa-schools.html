Overview

Bringing Finance to the
School Campus

FA @ Schools is Finance Academy's outreach initiative that partners with schools in Hosur and surrounding areas to deliver structured financial literacy programs, CA Foundation awareness workshops, and early career guidance sessions — embedded directly into the school curriculum calendar.

What We Offer

Programs for Schools

Financial Literacy Workshop

A 2-hour interactive session covering budgeting, savings, banking basics and the power of compounding — designed for Class 9–12 students.

CA Foundation Awareness Programme

A dedicated session for Class 11–12 Commerce students covering the CA pathway, exam structure, eligibility, and career outcomes — conducted by practicing CAs.

Finance Quiz & Competition

An inter-school finance quiz hosted by FA — building awareness, friendly competition, and recognition for finance-oriented students.

Why Partner with FA

Benefits for Schools

No Cost to Schools

FA absorbs all costs for awareness workshops — zero expense for the school.

Certified Faculty

All sessions are conducted by qualified CAs and finance professionals — not student volunteers.

Study Materials Provided

Students receive FA's printed financial literacy booklet — free to keep.

Career Counselling

1-on-1 guidance slots available post-session for students interested in finance careers.

Process

How It Works

01

02

FA co-ordinates with the school to find the best date and program format for students.

03

04
Follow-Up Support

Students get FA's contact info, a follow-up booklet, and access to free counselling sessions.
